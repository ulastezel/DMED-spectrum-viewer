## LIPID MAPS Structure Database (LMSD)

Compound names, structures, classes and NCBI taxonomy links come from the LIPID MAPS Structure Database (extended SDF export, 2026-10-15), lipidmaps.org, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data were transformed: in-silico hydrolysis, DMED derivatization and predicted MS/MS spectra were added.

## NCBI Taxonomy

Organism names and lineages were retrieved from the NCBI Taxonomy database through E-utilities.
